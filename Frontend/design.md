# Luxury Car Showroom UI Design Guide

## Design Direction

Style:

* Minimal
* Luxury
* Premium
* Dark Theme
* Cinematic
* Automotive

Target feeling:

```text
Modern
Powerful
High-end
Clean
Elegant
```

# Typography

## Recommended Fonts

### Heading

* Space Grotesk
* Montserrat
* Poppins

### Body

* Inter
* Manrope

## Best Combination

```text
Heading → Space Grotesk
Body → Inter
```

---

# UI Style

## Buttons

Use:

* rounded-xl
* subtle border
* dark surface
* gold hover

Avoid:

* neon colors
* childish gradients
* over-rounded buttons

---

# Cards

Product card should have:

* large car image
* minimal text
* wide spacing
* subtle shadow
* dark surface

Example:

```text
BMW M4 Competition
2024 • Automatic • AWD
$120,000
```

---

# Animations

Use:

* fade
* scale
* smooth transitions
* subtle hover effects

Avoid:

* bounce
* spinning
* flashy animations

Recommended:

* Framer Motion
* 200–300ms transitions

---

# Important Design Notes

## Focus on Images

Car showroom websites depend heavily on:

* high-quality car images
* cinematic photography
* dark backgrounds
* consistent lighting

Images contribute most of the luxury feeling.

# Final Recommendation

Keep the design:

* minimal
* dark
* premium
* image-focused
* spacious
* elegant

Avoid over-complicated UI and excessive colors.
